# Executive Summary
- This Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) evaluates data processing for ingesting and transforming customer and order data within the Application system.
- Focus is on Delta Live Tables ETL flows, personal data categories handled, security controls, risk identification, and regulatory compliance (GDPR, ISO 27701, NIST).

# Project Overview and Purpose of Processing
- Ingestion of customer/order data from CSVs to enterprise Delta tables.
- Cleansing, transformation with computed columns, historical (SCD Type 2) tracking, and aggregation for analytics.
- Purpose: analytics, reporting, and historical analysis.

# Description of Personal Data Categories Processed
- Customer Name
- EmailId
- Region
- Customer and Order Identifiers

# Lawful Basis for Processing
- Business analytics: legitimate interest or contractual necessity (GDPR Art. 6).
- Consent may be needed for downstream or repurposed use.

# Data Minimization Approach
- Only business-essential fields processed and stored.
- No collection of extraneous personal attributes.

# Consent Management Controls
- No explicit consent captured; relies on upstream source contractual/legal framework.
- Recommend integration with enterprise consent lifecycle and audit mechanisms.

# Third‑Party / Vendor Data Sharing Implications
- No direct external/vendor sharing.
- All processing handled within the organization's IT/data platform.

# Risk Identification
- Confidentiality: Potential exposure of PII if access is not restricted.
- Integrity: Erroneous transformations can corrupt analytics.
- Re‑identification: Historic and joined data increases risk.
- Access: Missing access controls could lead to unauthorized internal data use.

# Impact Assessment with Severity & Likelihood Scoring
- Confidentiality Breach: High severity, Medium likelihood.
- Data Corruption: Medium severity, Low likelihood with tested ETL.
- Excess Exposure (Historic/Join): Medium severity, Low likelihood.
- Improper Access: High severity, Medium likelihood in absence of RBAC.

# Mitigation Strategies & Security Controls (Encryption, DLP, IAM, Logging)
- Encryption at rest and in transit for all data tables.
- Role-based access on analytic and source Delta tables.
- DLP inspection and masking for analytics outputs.
- Audit logging for all table and reporting access.

# Residual Risk Evaluation
- Mostly controlled with standard security controls; residual risk considered low to medium post-mitigation.

# Compliance Mapping (GDPR Articles, Local Regulations)
- Art. 5, 6, 25, 32 (GDPR): purpose limitation, minimization, protection by design, and security controls reflected in ETL and platform setup.
- Local obligations to be validated per deployment region.

# Approval & Sign‑off Matrix
- Data Protection Officer: PIA and controls review.
- Data Owner: Acceptance of PIA and risk profile.
- Compliance/Legal: Regulatory feedback.
- Technical/Data Lead: Confirmation of implemented controls.
