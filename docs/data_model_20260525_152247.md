# Conceptual Data Model

- Entities:
  - Customer
    - Represents an individual customer with identity and contact details
  - Order
    - Represents a transaction or purchase made by a customer
  - OrderSummary
    - Represents an integrated view of customer and order information, maintaining the full change history (SCD Type 2)
  - CustomerAggregateSpend
    - Represents daily aggregated total spend per customer

- Key Relationships:
  - Customer "places" one or more Orders
  - OrderSummary is derived by joining Customer and Order on CustId (with SCD Type 2 tracking)
  - CustomerAggregateSpend aggregates OrderSummary by Name and Date

- Business Definitions:
  - Customer: The party purchasing goods or services
  - Order: A transactional record of a purchase or sale
  - OrderSummary: A historical fact table containing both customer and order details
  - CustomerAggregateSpend: The sum of all orders (by customer) per day

# Logical Data Model

- Entities, Attributes, Keys, Relationships:

  - Customer
    - CustId (Primary Key)
    - Name
    - EmailId
    - Region

  - Order
    - OrderId (Primary Key)
    - ItemName
    - PricePerUnit
    - Qty
    - Date
    - CustId (Foreign Key → Customer)

  - OrderSummary
    - CustId (Foreign Key → Customer)
    - Name
    - EmailId
    - Region
    - OrderId
    - ItemName
    - PricePerUnit
    - Qty
    - Date
    - (Implied SCD Type 2 fields: StartDate, EndDate, Active)

  - CustomerAggregateSpend
    - Name
    - TotalAmount
    - Date

- Cardinality and Relationships:
  - Customer 1 — * Order (one-to-many)
  - Order * — 1 Customer (many-to-one)
  - OrderSummary 1 — 1 Customer + 1 Order (composite join)
  - CustomerAggregateSpend * — 1 Name, 1 Date (aggregated facts)

- Normalization Notes:
  - Customer and Order are 3NF (no repeating groups, single facts per row)
  - OrderSummary denormalizes for historical tracking with SCD Type 2 (duplication expected)
  - CustomerAggregateSpend is an aggregate fact (denormalized for performance)

- Business Rules per Entity:
  - Customer: No duplicates, all fields cleansed for literal or "Null"/Null values
  - Order: Must be linked to Customer (CustId), no duplicates/"Null" values
  - OrderSummary: SCD Type 2—maintain full-history, status, active/inactive records
  - CustomerAggregateSpend: Summed TotalAmount grouped by Name and Date
