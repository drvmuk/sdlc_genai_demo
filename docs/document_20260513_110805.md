- Executive Summary
  - The document outlines business requirements for Application to process, cleanse, aggregate, and manage customer and order data sourced from CSV files, using persistent delta tables and automated workflows.

- Business Objectives
  - Efficiently ingest customer and order data from predefined volumes.
  - Clean data by removing nulls and duplicates.
  - Join customer and order datasets for unified views and history.
  - Aggregate total spend at customer and date level.
  - Automate workflow with delta live tables.

- In-Scope
  - Loading of CSV data for customer and order tables.
  - Schema definitions for all tables.
  - Data cleansing (null and duplication removal).
  - Computation of total amount in orders.
  - Building and maintaining order summary with SCD type 2.
  - Aggregating and storing customer spend.
  - Workflow orchestration via delta live tables.

- Out-of-Scope
  - Data ingestion from non-CSV sources.
  - Any downstream application processing outside specified tables.
  - Real-time event streaming and processing.
  - Advanced analytics or reporting layers.

- Business Requirements

### BR-SDL-001 — Load Customer Data from CSV
- Description: Ingest customer CSV data from specified volume into persistent delta table.
- Business Value: Centralizes customer information for downstream consumption.
- Priority: High

### BR-SDL-002 — Load Order Data from CSV
- Description: Ingest order CSV data from specified volume into persistent delta table.
- Business Value: Centralizes order information for unified analysis.
- Priority: High

### BR-SDL-003 — Data Cleansing for All Tables
- Description: Remove null and duplicate records from both customer and order tables.
- Business Value: Improves trust and accuracy for analytics.
- Priority: High

### BR-SDL-004 — Compute Total Amount in Orders
- Description: Add column "TotalAmount" to order table—computed as PricePerUnit * Qty.
- Business Value: Enables spend analysis and reporting.
- Priority: High

### BR-SDL-005 — Create and Maintain Order Summary with SCD Type 2
- Description: Join customer and order tables by CustId; load to ordersummary as SCD type 2 table, maintaining history.
- Business Value: Maintains change history for compliance and auditing.
- Priority: High

### BR-SDL-006 — Update Order Summary on Customer Changes (SCD Type 2 Logic)
- Description: Update ordersummary so old records become Inactive and new records Active; manage StartDate/EndDate for each version.
- Business Value: Ensures historical accuracy and lineage.
- Priority: High

### BR-SDL-007 — Aggregate Customer Spend
- Description: Aggregate TotalAmount by Name and Date from ordersummary table, load to customeraggregatespend table.
- Business Value: Provides insights into customer purchase behaviour over time.
- Priority: Medium

### BR-SDL-008 — Automate Workflow Using Delta Live Tables
- Description: Implement all ingestion, cleansing, join, and aggregation steps using delta live tables.
- Business Value: Orchestrates data pipeline for robustness and efficiency.
- Priority: High
