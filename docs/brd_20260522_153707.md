# Executive Summary
- This BRD details business requirements for ingesting customer and order CSV data into Delta tables, applying cleansing rules, computing a TotalAmount, creating an SCD Type 2 ordersummary, and aggregating daily customer spend, all orchestrated via Delta Live Tables.
- System Name: Application

# Business Objectives
- Enable reliable ingestion of customer and order CSV data into Delta tables.
- Enforce data cleansing by removing "Null"/Null literals and duplicates.
- Enrich order records with TotalAmount (PricePerUnit × Qty).
- Establish ordersummary table with SCD Type 2 change management for customers.
- Aggregate spend per customer per date in customeraggregatespend.
- Standardize pipeline steps using Delta Live Tables.

# In-Scope
- Ingestion of customer and order CSV files from defined paths.
- Creation and population of Delta tables: customer, order, ordersummary, customeraggregatespend.
- Application of data cleansing rules.
- Calculation of TotalAmount.

- Aggregation to customeraggregatespend.
- Implementation using Delta Live Tables.

# Business Requirements

### BR-ING-001 — Source Data Ingestion
- Description: Load customer and order CSV files into corresponding Delta tables from specified filesystem paths.
- Business Value: Ensures controlled and auditable data onboarding pipeline.
- Priority: High

### BR-SCH-002 — Schema Enforcement
- Description: Apply defined schemas to customer (CustId, Name, EmailId, Region) and order (OrderId, ItemName, PricePerUnit, Qty, Date, CustId) tables.
- Business Value: Enforces structure and supports data integrity.
- Priority: High

### BR-CLE-003 — Data Cleansing
- Description: Remove "Null"/Null literals and duplicate records from customer and order tables.
- Business Value: Delivers quality inputs for downstream analytics.
- Priority: High

### BR-COM-004 — Computed TotalAmount
- Description: Calculate TotalAmount as PricePerUnit × Qty for each order.
- Business Value: Adds essential metric for spend analysis.
- Priority: High

### BR-JOI-005 — Customer-Order Join
- Description: Join customer and order data on CustId to create an ordersummary table.
- Business Value: Centralizes transactional context for analysis.
- Priority: High

### BR-SCD-006 — SCD Type 2 Customer Dimension
- Description: Implement SCD Type 2 logic in ordersummary for historical customer attribute management (Active/Inactive, StartDate/EndDate, full history).
- Business Value: Supports time-travel reporting and regulatory compliance.
- Priority: High

### BR-AGG-007 — Customer Aggregate Spend
- Description: Compute and store daily aggregate spend (sum of TotalAmount by Name and Date) in customeraggregatespend table.
- Business Value: Enables trend and behavior analytics.
- Priority: High

### BR-DEP-008 — Delta Live Tables Implementation
- Description: Execute the entire pipeline (steps 1–10) using Delta Live Tables as orchestration and ETL engine.
- Business Value: Ensures reliability, automation, and alignment with modern data stack.
- Priority: High
