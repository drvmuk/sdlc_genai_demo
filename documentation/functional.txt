**FR-FIN-001: Populate Finance Table with Transformed Data**

**Title:** Populate Finance Table with Transformed Data from Everest ECC Source Tables

**Description:** The system must transform and load data from Everest ECC source tables (FAGLFLEXA and BSEG) into the Finance table, applying specified business rules and filters.

**Preconditions:**

* The source tables (FAGLFLEXA and BSEG) are available and contain required data.
* The Fiscal Year and Posting Period parameters are provided.
* The Golden Entity, GL, and Trading Partner views are available.

**Main Flow / Functional Steps:**

1. **Retrieve Source Data:**
	* Join FAGLFLEXA with BSEG on document number, fiscal year, and company code for records with XBILK = 'X'.
	* Apply filter RLDNR = '0L'.
2. **Apply Filters and Derive Fields:**
	* Filter out records with CompCode starting with '8%'.
	* Derive Legal Entity by reading Entity golden view and exclude archived Golden Entity values.
	* Derive GLAccount by selecting distinct FAGLFLEXA.DOCNR where FAGLFLEXA.RACCT belongs to golden acct realized and unrealized.
	* Derive GoldenGLAcct by converting FAGLFLEXA.RACCT to golden GL account.
	* Derive TradingPartner and GoldenTradingPartner by reading respective golden views.
3. **Calculate GainLossGC and GainLossLC:**
	* Calculate GainLossGC using BPC exchange rates (s_shared.v_actual_exchange_rate_bpc) and FiscalYearPeriod.
	* Derive GainLossLC by applying logic based on LocalCurrency.
4. **Derive Additional Fields:**
	* Derive GainLossTC by applying logic based on TransactionCurrency.
	* Derive OffsetAccount by applying logic for Realized and Unrealized accounts.
	* Derive GoldenOffsetAccount by converting OffsetAccount to golden GL account.
5. **Load Transformed Data into Finance Table:**
	* Populate the Finance table with transformed data, including derived fields.

**Detailed Field Derivation Rules:**

* FiscalYear: FY parameter
* PostingPeriod: Posting Period parameter
* SourceFiscalYear: FAGLFLEXA.RYEAR = FY Parameter
* SourcePeriod: FAGLFLEXA.POPER = Posting Period parameter
* DocumentNumber: FAGLFLEXA.DOCNR
* CompCode: FAGLFLEXA.RBUKRS (excluding those starting with '8%')
* LocalCurrency: derived from Entity view (GFA Revenue)
* TransactionCurrency: FAGLFLEXA.RWCUR
* OffsetAccount: derived based on logic for Realized and Unrealized accounts
* Offset Account LC Amount: BSEG.DMBTR
* Offset Account TC Amount: BSEG.WRBTR
* Offset Clearing Document Number: BSEG.AUGBL
* SourceSystem: 'ECC Everest'
